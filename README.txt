Adee Foods homepage demo

Files:
- index.html: page structure and product content
- style.css: responsive design and visual styling
- script.js: product filtering, search, demo cart and newsletter interaction

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or upload all three files to your web hosting.
3. Internet is needed for Google Fonts and the sample Unsplash product images.

Important:
- Product prices and product photos are demo placeholders; update them to match your actual stock and prices.
- Add-to-cart is a front-end demo only. Checkout, payments, accounts, order tracking, email collection, and database inventory require a backend or ecommerce platform.
